Charl's
Bistro

Charl's Bistro is one of Tirana's most iconic pubs, located in the popular Blloku area for over 17 years. Inspired by the retro style of the 60-80s, it serves as a cozy bistro during the day and transforms into an energetic nightclub on the weekends. Known for its legendary Live Rock nights, Latin themed nights and a wide selection of beers and cocktails, Charl's Bistro remains the capital's favorite destination for real fun.
